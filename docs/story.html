Why monaco-json-studio-base-header Exists

Handlebars (.hbs) header region primitives paired with dom-primitive-bindings.

Design Story

Headers carry labels, file pickers, dataset dropdowns, and toolbar buttons that evolve independently from editor panes or footers.

By separating header markup into Handlebars templates (title.hbs, file-picker.hbs, select.hbs, actions.hbs) and wiring events declaratively with dom-primitive-bindings (bindings.json + actions.js), zero HTML lives inside JavaScript.
